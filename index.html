Everything stays here.

A full keyboard, with the clever parts running on your phone instead of someone's server.

01

Type, swipe, predict.

Autocorrect, next-word predictions and swipe typing, worked out on your phone. Emoji search works in every language.

02

Speak. It types, offline.

Voice transcription in English that works with no connection at all. NVIDIA Parakeet turns your speech into text right on your phone. Audio stays in memory and is never saved or sent.

The voice pack is a separate 662 MB download. Your browser fetches it and you import it, so BooBoard itself never goes online.

03

Translate as you type.

Offline translation packs, right in the keyboard. English to Spanish works today.

More directions (French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Russian) are planned.

04

A clipboard that keeps quiet.

Clipboard history with pins. Learned words, phrases and clips are encrypted with a key held in Android Keystore, and never synced.

05

Incognito, automatically.

Nothing is learned in password fields, browser incognito tabs, or apps you pick. Add app lock and hide the keyboard from screenshots.

Eighteen languages, one keyboard.

Turn on several at once. Hindi works in Devanagari, InScript or English letters, Hinglish included. Japanese has 12-key flick or romaji.

Coming in a later update

Pictures, made on your phone.

A later update adds offline image generation: describe a picture and BooBoard makes it right on your phone, with Stable Diffusion 1.5 or fine-tuned models in an optional pack. Like everything in BooBoard, it will work offline.
